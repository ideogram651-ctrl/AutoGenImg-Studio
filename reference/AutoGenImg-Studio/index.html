Beginner’s Guided Workflow: Script to Prompt Bundle

Follow these 7 steps to prepare your script, structure your prompts, and configure automated generation.

Step 1: Prepare Your Video Script as Plain Text

Have your video narration, voiceover, or screen action formatted in a clean plain-text .txt file. The AI prompt engineer will analyze this text to partition it into logical scene beats.

Best Practice:
  • Include timing timestamps if you have recorded voiceover (e.g. [00:00 - 00:05]).
  • Remove technical sound effects or stage directions that should not be depicted visually.
  • Clearly mention character names whenever they appear in a scene.
Step 1 of 7

Step 2: Select a Visual Direction

Choose one cohesive visual aesthetic for your entire bundle so all generated shots look like parts of the same production.

Raw / Cinematic 35mm

Most Popular for YouTube Video Essays

Volumetric natural lighting, organic film grain, shallow depth of field, anamorphic lens flares, real camera angles.

Photorealistic Humans

Explainer & Case Studies

Studio lighting, high texture detail, natural skin pores, realistic expressions, candid documentary style.

Realistic 3D CGI

Animated Shorts & Tech Demos

Octane renderer aesthetics, clean metallic shaders, stylized character geometry, vibrant color palettes.

Minimalist Stickman / Diagrammatic

Fast Paced Educational Content

High contrast black ink lines on textured cream background, clear visual metaphors, playful hand-drawn feel.

Step 2 of 7

Step 3: Define Image Timing & Plan Calculations

Specify your video duration and pacing interval. The application uses transparent ceiling arithmetic to ensure every second of footage is covered by a visual scene.

Rule: Total Images = Math.ceil(durationSeconds / intervalSeconds)

For example: A 120-second video with an image every 5 seconds requires exactly 24 images (IMG-001 to IMG-024).

If duration is 122 seconds with a 5s interval: 24 full 5s intervals + 1 final partial 2s interval = 25 images.

Step 3 of 7

Step 4: Prepare Visual References

Reference files allow AI image generators to lock in a specific face, wardrobe, environment architecture, or color grade across dozens of prompts.

Style References (e.g. REF-STYLE-01)

Applied to All Prompts. Defines lighting tone, film grain, color grading, and camera sensor parameters.

Character References (e.g. REF-CHAR-01)

Applied selectively only to prompts where that specific character appears (e.g. IMG-001, IMG-003, IMG-005).
